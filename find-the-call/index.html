This page lists calls for papers in journals on the 2025 ABDC Journal Quality List, filterable by rating and field of research.

How it reads "Opens" and "Closes"
  • Not stated: the publisher doesn't say when the call opened. It is listed as open now.
  • Ongoing: the publisher lists the call with no closing date.
  • A date: taken from the publisher's page. A count-down shows when it is 30 days or fewer away.
Coverage

Calls for papers are collated from information that is publicly available on the web, including some added manually. The list is refreshed monthly. A journal with no call listed may still have one open.

Generated with AI

This page and its data were assembled with the help of AI, which can make mistakes. Ratings, call details, dates and links may be incomplete, wrong or out of date. Always confirm on the journal's own homepage before you plan or submit.
